Get Involved

Connect with national committees and seva initiatives.
The 6th Principle of Arya Samaj
“The prime object of the Arya Samaj is to do good to the world, i.e., to ameliorate physical, spiritual and social welfare of all.”

The Arya Pratinidhi Sabha America (APSA) is driven by the passion, intellect, and selfless service (Seva) of our community members. From organizing the annual Mahasammelan to leading disaster relief efforts, our work is only possible when we come together. Whether you can offer your professional skills, your time, or your financial support, there is a place for you to make a meaningful impact.

✿ Volunteer Pathways

Ways to Give Back

Discover how your specialized skills, dedication, and compassionate spirit can elevate our community across North America.

1. Join a National Committee

APSA operates across North America through dedicated volunteer committees. We are always looking for passionate individuals to contribute their professional skills.

Current Needs
  • IT & Website Management
  • Marketing & Communications
  • Navrang Times Editorial Support
  • Event Planning (Mahasammelan)
  • Legal & Governance

2. Seva & Humanitarian Relief

APSA has a long legacy of answering the call during global crises. Join our Seva network to help mobilize resources for natural disaster relief and support child welfare charities.

Current Needs
  • Fundraising Coordinators
  • Local Relief Drive Organizers
  • On-the-Ground Coordination
  • Jeevan Prabhat Center Support

3. Mentor the Next Generation

Help us bridge timeless Vedic wisdom with modern life by empowering our youth. Our educational initiatives are constantly growing and require dedicated mentors and teachers.

Current Needs
  • Vedic Sanskriti School (VSS) Coordinators
  • VDAT National Exam Proctors
  • VAYU Youth Camp Counselors
  • Balwadi Online Guides
