Constitution & Governance

Our organizational bylaws and commitment to transparency.

The Arya Pratinidhi Sabha America (APSA) operates as a registered not-for-profit organization with absolute transparency, integrity, and democratic governance.

Democratic Accountability

Guided by the ethical standards laid out by Maharshi Dayanand Saraswati, our Executive Committee and Board of Directors ensure fair elections, regular assembly meetings, and democratic decision-making.

Financial Integrity

All operations and funds are strictly directed toward youth education, charitable disaster relief, community seva initiatives, and the preservation of Vedic Dharma.

Standardized Bylaws

Our constitution defines the qualifications for affiliation, officer duties, and regional representation across all North American chapters and Vedic youth institutions.

Review Our Bylaws

We invite all community members, chapter delegates, and supporters to review the official APSA Constitution to understand our structural framework, voting procedures, and operational guidelines.
