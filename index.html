Spirituality grounded in truth and action

The Arya Samaj does not just study the Vedas — we apply their universal truths to solve modern problems. Explore the ten principles that guide every member.

God is the primary source of all true knowledge and of all that is known by its means. He is the efficient cause of the universe, all-knowing, eternal, and without form.

God is existent, conscious and all-blissful. He is formless, omnipotent, just, merciful, unborn, infinite, unchangeable, beginningless, incomparable, the support of all, the lord of all, all-pervading, omniscient, imperishable, immortal, exempt from fear, eternal, holy and the maker of the universe. To Him alone worship is due.

The Vedas are the scriptures of true knowledge. It is the paramount duty of every Arya to read them, to teach them to others, to hear them, and to recite them to others.

One should always be ready to accept truth and renounce untruth. All actions should be performed in conformity with Dharma, that is, after due reflection upon what is right and wrong.

All acts should be performed in accordance with Dharma, that is, after due consideration of truth and falsehood, right and wrong. Dharma is the path of righteousness that leads to spiritual progress.

The prime object of the Arya Samaj is to do good to the whole world, that is, to ameliorate the physical, spiritual, and social conditions of all humankind.

Our conduct towards all should be guided by love, by righteousness, and by justice. We should treat others as we would want to be treated ourselves.

We should dispel Avidya (ignorance) and promote Vidya (true knowledge).

No one should be content with promoting their own welfare only, but should look for it in the welfare of all.

One should subordinate oneself to the rules of society calculated to promote the well-being of all, while remaining free to follow the rules of individual welfare.
