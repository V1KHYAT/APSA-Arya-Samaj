✿ ABOUT APSA ✿
ROOTED IN THE VEDAS. COMMITTED TO HUMANITY.

An ancient foundation. A living movement.

✿

Arya Pratinidhi Sabha America (APSA) brings together Arya Samajs and Vedic organizations across North America to preserve our heritage and build a better world.

01
OUR

Mission

To unite and empower Arya Samajs and Vedic organizations across North America in preserving, living, and propagating the eternal values of Vedic Dharma.

Rooted in the teachings of Maharishi Dayanand Saraswati and guided by the Ten Principles of Arya Samaj, we strive to dispel ignorance, foster social equality, promote education, and work continuously for the physical, spiritual, and social well-being of all humanity.

✿
02
OUR

Vision

To build a thriving, dynamic legacy across North America that empowers individuals and families to live Vedic principles daily.

We aim to bridge our rich cultural heritage with modern life and lead global efforts in service, education, and social reform.

A Legacy of Unity and Service

Our History

The Arya Pratinidhi Sabha America (APSA) was established to connect and empower Arya Samaj members across the continent.

1988

A Continental Vision

The Arya Pratinidhi Sabha America (APSA) traces its roots to 1988, when Girish Khosla began connecting with Arya Samaj members across North America through correspondence. His vision was to unite a scattered diaspora into a cohesive community dedicated to the principles of the Vedas.

1991

The First Mahasammelan

His efforts, supported by visionary leaders like Lala Ram Chand Mahajan, Pt Ramlal, and Amar Erry, culminated in the first Arya Mahasammelan on April 13, 1991, in Detroit, Michigan. This landmark event gathered Aryas under one roof, officially establishing APSA as an umbrella organization dedicated to promoting and preserving Vedic values across the continent.

Service

Global Humanitarian Relief

Since its inception, APSA has grown into a cohesive family that holds annual Mahasammelans across North America and remains deeply committed to charitable service. The organization has provided vital support during global crises, including earthquakes in Gujarat, Haiti, and Nepal, the South India tsunami, hurricanes Irma and Harvey, and massive COVID-19 relief efforts in India.

Today

A Thriving Legacy

Today, guided by the legacy of Maharshi Dayanand Saraswati and sustained by a new generation of leadership, APSA continues its mission. We strive to unite forces, uplift society, and pursue the universal vision of making the world noble.

THE PRINCIPLES OF ARYA SAMAJ ✿

The Ten Niyams

The foundation of the Arya Samaj is built upon ten core principles that emphasize truth, logic, and universal welfare.

कृण्वन्तो विश्वमार्यम् ।
आ नो भद्राः क्रतवो यन्तु विश्वतः ॥

ओ३म्
01

Eeshwar

The first (efficient) cause of all true knowledge and all that is known through knowledge is Eeshwar (the Supreme, i.e. GOD).

02

The Nature of God

Eeshwar (GOD) is existent, intelligent, and blissful. He is formless, omniscient, just, merciful, unborn, endless, and the support of all.

03

The Vedas

The Vedas are the scriptures of all true knowledge. It is the paramount duty of Aryas to read them, teach them, recite them, and hear them.

04

Truth Over Untruth

One should always be ready to accept truth and renounce untruth.

05

Act According to Dharma

All acts ought to be performed in conformity to Dharma i.e., after deliberating what is right and wrong.

06

Welfare of All

The prime object of the Arya Samaj is to do good to the world, i.e., to ameliorate physical, spiritual and social welfare of all.

07

Treat All with Respect

All ought to be treated with affection, justice, righteousness and due regard to their merits.

08

Pursuit of Knowledge

One should endeavor to promote knowledge and to dispel ignorance.

09

Self-Study & Dissemination

One ought to read one's own welfare and that of others by self-study, and should disseminate that knowledge.

10

Order & Discipline

All social dealings should be regulated by love and righteousness.
