Daily Practices

Sandhya — Morning & Evening Prayer

Sandhya is the twice-daily prayer practice of Arya Samaj, performed at dawn and dusk. It involves mantra recitation, pranayama, and meditation — a cornerstone of Vedic daily life.

Agnihotra — Daily Yajna & Havan

Daily havan (fire oblation) is the essence of Arya Samaj practice — purifying the environment, mind, and spirit through Vedic mantras and ahuti. Guides for home practice included.

Services

Purohit Services

APSA maintains a directory of trained Vedic purohits available to conduct samskaras and religious ceremonies — from Namkaran and Upanayana to Vivah and Antyesti.

All APSA-affiliated purohits are trained in the Vedic tradition, and ceremonies are conducted strictly according to Arya Samaj methodology — without superstition, idol worship, or unscriptural rites.

Ceremonies Covered
  • • Namkaran (Naming)
  • • Mundan (Hair)
  • • Upanayana (Sacred Thread)
  • • Vivah (Marriage)
  • • Grih Pravesh (Housewarming)
  • • Antyesti (Last Rites)
  • • Shraddha (Remembrance)
  • • Satyanarayan Havan
